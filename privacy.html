Privacy Policy

Su Huang Kitchen (the "App") — last updated: 7 November 2026.

What the App does

The App is a private tool used only by its owner to schedule and upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to read the owner's own Google Sheet that contains the posting schedule. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the App accesses: (1) the YouTube account of the owner, only to upload and schedule videos; (2) a Google Sheet owned by the owner, only to read the posting schedule and to write a status note ("posted") back to it.

Data we collect and store

The App does not collect personal data from any other person. Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sent to third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
